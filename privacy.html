What Health Bridge does

Health Bridge is an Android utility that reads permitted Android Health Connect data, prepares reports for user-selected AI assistants and writes nutrition or body-measurement records. No Health Bridge account is required.

Data processed

Depending on your permissions and actions, the app processes Health Connect records, nutrition entries, app preferences, locally stored sync history and diagnostics, selected AI chat links, and optional Zepp Life credentials and body measurements.

Sharing and recipients

Health Connect information is handled on your device and in Health Connect. When you choose to share information with an AI assistant, selected information may leave the device and be processed under that provider's privacy policy. Health Bridge does not operate a server that receives your Health Connect records.

Optional Zepp Life import sends your supplied account identifier and authentication token directly to Zepp's HTTPS service to retrieve measurements. The web link bridge is hosted on GitHub Pages. Nutrition data in the fragment portion of a link is not transmitted to the web host in an HTTP request; full links may still be visible to AI providers, browsers, chat histories and screenshots. Previously generated legacy query links may have exposed nutrition values in web requests; the current bridge rejects query-based imports.

Analytics and notifications

The reviewed app does not include an application usage analytics or crash-reporting SDK. App-open telemetry is not implemented. Experimental Galaxy Fit 3 notifications are generated locally; their behavior depends on Android and Samsung software.

Storage and deletion

Preferences, import history and diagnostics are stored locally. Optional Zepp credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore. You can remove app data using Android settings, revoke Health Connect permissions, and manage Health Connect records in Health Connect. External AI providers and Zepp manage their own retention under their policies.

Security

The app disables Android backup and cleartext network traffic. Zepp requests use HTTPS. No security measure guarantees absolute protection.

Your choices and rights

You can choose which Health Connect permissions to grant, revoke them in Android settings, and decide whether to share information with an AI provider. For privacy questions or applicable data protection requests, contact v.zavershinskii@gmail.com.
